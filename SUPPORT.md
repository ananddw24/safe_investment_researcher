# Support

FinancialLibrary is **educational only**. Maintainers cannot and will not give
personalized financial, tax, or investment advice.

## How to get help

| Need | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Bug in docs build, CI, or scripts | [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/ananddw24/safe_investment_researcher/issues) — use the Bug template |
| Incorrect educational claim | Issues — Content correction template |
| Suggest a curated resource or vehicle | Issues — New resource / New vehicle templates |
| General contribution questions | Open an issue referencing [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) |

## What we will not do

- Recommend products for your personal situation
- Help open brokerage or bank accounts
- Provide a private “ask me anything about my money” channel

Discussions may be enabled later; until then, use Issues for project topics only.
