# Educational disclaimer

!!! danger "Not financial advice"
    FinancialLibrary provides **general educational information** about money
    basics, regulated savings and investment *types*, and public resources.
    Nothing on this site is personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal
    advice. Nothing here is an offer to buy, sell, or hold any product, or an
    invitation to open an account.

## Always verify

- Rules, eligibility, fees, and documentation requirements differ by country
  and change over time.
- Prefer primary sources: central banks, securities regulators, treasuries,
  ministries, and official government portals.
- Be wary of anyone promising “guaranteed high returns” with little risk —
  especially unregulated schemes.

## No accounts, no KYC, no sales

This project is a static documentation site plus curated link metadata. We do
not collect your financial data, open accounts, or sell products.
