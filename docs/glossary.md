# Glossary

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

Short definitions for terms used on this site. For reading order, see
[Start here on the homepage](index.md#start-here).

## Emergency fund

Money kept for sudden needs (illness, job gap, urgent travel). It should be
easy to reach without large penalties. See
[Emergency savings](guides/emergency-savings.md).

## Inflation

When many goods and services cost more over time, the same cash buys less.
A bank rate can look positive while purchasing power still falls after
[inflation](guides/inflation-capital-preservation.md) and fees.

## Liquidity

How quickly you can turn savings into spendable cash without a large cost.
High liquidity means easier access. Low liquidity means waiting or paying a
penalty.

## Capital preservation

Trying to keep the money you already have from being lost to scams, hidden
fees, or needless risk — not the same as chasing the highest return. See
[Inflation and capital preservation](guides/inflation-capital-preservation.md).

## Regulated

A product or provider that sits under a named official authority (for example
a central bank or securities regulator). Regulation does **not** mean
risk-free. Always confirm the licence on the official site.

## Unregulated

A tip, club, app, or collector that cannot show a clear licence on an official
domain. High “guaranteed” returns with little risk are a classic warning sign.
See [Spotting unregulated schemes](guides/spotting-unregulated-schemes.md).

## Deposit insurance

A government-backed scheme that may protect eligible bank deposits up to a
limit if a bank fails. Limits and coverage differ by country. Check the
deposit insurer named for your country in the
[resource catalog](resources/catalog.md).

## Vehicle (on this site)

A *type* of savings or investment product households may encounter (bank
deposit, pension concept, mobile-money wallet, and similar). Pages describe
how the type typically works — not which product to buy. See
[Vehicles](vehicles/index.md).
