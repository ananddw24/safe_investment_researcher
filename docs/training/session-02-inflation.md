# Session 2 — Inflation and capital preservation (75 minutes)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

## Who this is for

Community trainers facilitating workshops for **low-income households and
women**, after Session 1 or with a group that already understands emergency
cash.

## Learning objectives

By the end, participants can:

1. Explain [inflation](../glossary.md#inflation) as “the same cash buys less.”
2. Contrast “looks like a positive rate” with purchasing power after fees.
3. Describe [capital preservation](../glossary.md#capital-preservation) as a
   goal type — not a product tip.
4. Open an official statistics or central-bank page without copying a tipster’s
   number.

## Timed steps (75 minutes)

| Minutes | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Welcome. Read the [Educational disclaimer](../disclaimer.md). |
| 5–15 | Recall Session 1: why emergency cash stays accessible. |
| 15–35 | Walk through [Inflation and capital preservation](../guides/inflation-capital-preservation.md): nominal vs real, horizon, unstable contexts. |
| 35–45 | Glossary: [inflation](../glossary.md#inflation), [capital preservation](../glossary.md#capital-preservation). |
| 45–60 | Pairs open [Where to check official figures](../about/where-to-check.md) for their country. Find the publisher. **Do not write a rate on the board unless someone reads it live from the official site.** Prefer “here is where to look.” |
| 60–70 | Discussion: when is “don’t lose the money to a scam” more important than chasing a higher number? |
| 70–75 | Close: remind the group this page is not a forecast. |

## Discussion prompts

- What household costs have risen in ways people notice week to week?
- Why can moving emergency money into a market product after inflation anxiety be risky?
- How do you spot a tip that uses yesterday’s scheme rate against today’s prices?

## Do not

!!! danger "Facilitator do-not box"
    - Do not invent or guess an inflation percentage
    - Do not say which product “beats inflation”
    - Do not forecast rates or currency moves
    - Do not treat any figure from social media as official
