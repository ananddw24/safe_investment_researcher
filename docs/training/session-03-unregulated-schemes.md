# Session 3 — Spotting unregulated schemes (75 minutes)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

## Who this is for

Community trainers facilitating workshops for **low-income households and
women**, especially groups exposed to WhatsApp tips, doorstep collectors, or
“guaranteed return” clubs.

## Learning objectives

By the end, participants can:

1. Name the classic pitch: high return with “no risk.”
2. Explain [regulated](../glossary.md#regulated) vs
   [unregulated](../glossary.md#unregulated) in plain words.
3. List pressure and secrecy red flags from the guide.
4. Practice asking “which regulator?” and verifying on an official domain.

## Timed steps (75 minutes)

| Minutes | Step |
| --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Welcome. Read the [Educational disclaimer](../disclaimer.md). |
| 5–20 | Role-play a fictional tipster pitch (no real product names). Group shouts red flags. |
| 20–40 | Walk through [Spotting unregulated schemes](../guides/spotting-unregulated-schemes.md). |
| 40–50 | Glossary: [regulated](../glossary.md#regulated) vs [unregulated](../glossary.md#unregulated). |
| 50–65 | In pairs: open one official regulator or central-bank page from the [resource catalog](../resources/catalog.md). Write the organisation name — not a product tip. |
| 65–75 | Close: optional preview of the [Woman-led household pathway](../pathways/woman-led-household.md) as a later discussion on access and caregivers. |

## Discussion prompts

- What does “government backed” mean if you cannot find it on a `.gov` / official domain?
- How can recruitment pressure (“bring three friends today”) hide risk?
- What paperwork should people keep, and what should they refuse to hand over blindly?

## Do not

!!! danger "Facilitator do-not box"
    - Do not accuse a named neighbour or local shop without evidence
    - Do not recommend a replacement product or app
    - Do not claim regulated products are risk-free
    - Do not collect money, IDs, or group subscriptions in the workshop
