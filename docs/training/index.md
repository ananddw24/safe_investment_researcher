# Community training

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

These **75-minute** session plans help community trainers run workshops for
**low-income households and women**. Each plan uses only pages that already
exist in this library. Plans do not invent products, prices, or “what to buy.”

## Optional fourth discussion

After Sessions 1–3, you may open the
[Woman-led household pathway](../pathways/woman-led-household.md) as a
**discussion**, not a new curriculum. Use it to talk about access, caregivers,
and reading order — not to sell products.

## Facilitator checklist

1. Read the [Educational disclaimer](../disclaimer.md) aloud at the start.
2. Print or open the session plan and the linked guide.
3. Keep phones for looking up **official** sites from
   [Where to check official figures](../about/where-to-check.md) — not tipster apps.
4. Point unfamiliar words to the [Glossary](../glossary.md).

## Do not

- Do not recommend a bank, fund, app, or contribution amount
- Do not collect KYC or open accounts in the workshop
- Do not promise returns or “safe high yield”
- Do not treat this library as personalized advice
