# Pathway: woman-led household

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## Who this path is for

Women and caregivers who manage day-to-day money, may share or seek account
access, and want a **reading order** — not a personal financial plan.

## Ordered steps

1. Read [Emergency savings](../guides/emergency-savings.md).
2. Skim [Spotting unregulated schemes](../guides/spotting-unregulated-schemes.md).
3. Learn the foundation account: [Bank savings (India)](../vehicles/in/bank-savings.md).
4. If group finance is part of your community, read
   [SHG / microfinance literacy](../vehicles/in/shg-microfinance.md) carefully —
   focus on risks and regulation questions.
5. For long girl-child goals discussed in official scheme materials, see
   [Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana](../vehicles/in/sukanya-samriddhi.md).
6. For long household horizons after a cash buffer exists, optionally read
   [PPF](../vehicles/in/ppf.md) and [India Post schemes](../vehicles/in/postal-schemes.md).
7. Revisit [Inflation and capital preservation](../guides/inflation-capital-preservation.md)
   before any market-linked product literacy such as
   [SIP / ELSS](../vehicles/in/sip-elss.md) or [NPS](../vehicles/in/nps.md).

## What this path does not do

- Does not recommend purchases or contribution amounts
- Does not open accounts or collect KYC
- Does not replace official advice in your jurisdiction

## Next verification tips

- Prefer sites ending in known government / regulator domains
- Confirm scheme interest and limits on the latest official notification
- Keep nominees and paper copies of account openings
