# Pathway: new reader

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

## Who this path is for

Anyone opening FinancialLibrary for the first time who wants a **short,
country-neutral** reading order before diving into local product types.

## Ordered steps

1. Read the [Educational disclaimer](../disclaimer.md) (two minutes).
2. [Emergency savings](../guides/emergency-savings.md) — why accessible cash
   comes before long lock-ins.
3. [Spotting unregulated schemes](../guides/spotting-unregulated-schemes.md) —
   red flags that show up in every market.
4. Open [Vehicles](../vehicles/index.md) and choose **your country**. Skim that
   country’s index page, then read one page on the most familiar channel (often
   bank deposits or mobile money).
5. Open the [resource catalog](../resources/catalog.md) for your country and
   bookmark one **regulator or government** link to verify details later.
6. When you are ready for longer horizons, read
   [Inflation and capital preservation](../guides/inflation-capital-preservation.md).
