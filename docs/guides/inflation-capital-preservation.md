# Inflation and capital preservation

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

## What this is

[Inflation](../glossary.md#inflation) means many goods and services cost more
over time, so the **same cash buys less**. This guide teaches the idea.

## What this is not

It does not tell you which product “beats inflation.” It is not a forecast.

New words: [inflation](../glossary.md#inflation) ·
[capital preservation](../glossary.md#capital-preservation).

## Core ideas

1. **Nominal vs real.** A deposit rate can look positive while purchasing power
   still falls after inflation and fees.
2. **Preservation is a goal type.** Sometimes the literacy goal is “don’t lose
   the principal in a scam,” not “maximize return.”
3. **Horizon matters.** Money needed next month has different constraints than
   money needed in fifteen years.
4. **Unstable contexts.** When prices move quickly, short accessible buffers and
   skepticism toward “guaranteed high returns” both matter. See
   [Spotting unregulated schemes](spotting-unregulated-schemes.md).

## Risks and red flags

- Comparing yesterday’s scheme rate to today’s prices without checking official
  notifications.
- Moving emergency money into volatile market products to “catch up” after
  inflation anxiety.
- Cross-border tipsters promising FX miracles.
