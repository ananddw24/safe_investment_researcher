# Spotting unregulated “guaranteed return” schemes

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

## What this is

This guide lists **common red flags** for
[unregulated](../glossary.md#unregulated) tips and clubs.

## What this is not

It is not a complete legal test. It does not promise that
[regulated](../glossary.md#regulated) products are risk-free.

New words: [regulated](../glossary.md#regulated) ·
[unregulated](../glossary.md#unregulated).

## Core ideas

1. **“Guaranteed high return with no risk”** is the classic pitch — especially on
   WhatsApp, Telegram, or doorstep collectors.
2. **Ask which regulator.** Banks, funds, and pensions usually sit under named
   authorities. Verify the name on the official site.
3. **Pressure and secrecy.** “Recruit three friends,” “limited slots today,”
   and “don’t tell the bank” are danger signs.
4. **Paperwork.** Refuse to hand over original ID without understanding why.
   Prefer written contracts you can keep.

## Risks and red flags

- Returns far above ordinary bank deposit rates with “government backing” claims
  that you cannot find on an official domain.
- Collectors who mix savings-group language with personal lending.
- Celebrity or deepfake endorsements.
- Crypto or forex “savings clubs” with no regulated intermediary.
