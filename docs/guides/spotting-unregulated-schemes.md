# Spotting unregulated “guaranteed return” schemes

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What this is / isn’t

This guide lists **common red flags**. It is not a complete legal test and not a
promise that regulated products are risk-free.

## Core ideas

1. **“Guaranteed high return with no risk”** is the classic pitch — especially on
   WhatsApp, Telegram, or doorstep collectors.
2. **Licensing questions** — banks, NBFCs, mutual funds, and pensions sit under
   named regulators; ask which one and verify on the official site.
3. **Pressure and secrecy** — “recruit three friends,” “limited slots today,”
   “don’t tell the bank” are danger signs.
4. **Paperwork** — refuse to hand over originals of ID without understanding why;
   prefer written contracts you can keep.

## Risks & red flags

- Returns far above ordinary bank deposit rates with “government backing” claims
  that cannot be found on an official domain.
- Collectors who mix SHG language with personal lending.
- Celebrity or deepfake endorsements.
- Crypto or forex “savings clubs” with no regulated intermediary.

## Where to verify

- SEBI investor alerts / investor portal
- RBI warnings and financial education
- NCFE and police cybercrime portals when fraud has already occurred

Links: [SEBI Investor Portal](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/),
[RBI Financial Education](https://www.rbi.org.in/FinancialEducation/),
[NCFE](https://ncfe.org.in/).
