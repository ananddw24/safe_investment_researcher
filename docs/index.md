# FinancialLibrary

**FinancialLibrary** helps **low-income households** and **women** learn money
basics. It explains regulated savings and investment *types* by country. It
points to trusted public resources.

This site does not open accounts. It does not sell products. It does not give
personalized advice.

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

## Who this is for

- People building a first cash buffer ([emergency fund](glossary.md#emergency-fund))
- Women and caregivers managing household money
- Community trainers and NGOs who need cite-first materials

See [Audience](about/audience.md) and
[Using this library](about/using-this-library.md).

## What's here

- **15 countries, 94 vehicle pages** — the savings and investment *types*
  households actually meet, each tied to its official regulator.
- **81 curated official resources** in the
  [resource catalog](resources/catalog.md), each showing the date it was
  last checked.
- **3 literacy guides** (emergency savings, inflation, spotting
  unregulated schemes), **3 community training sessions**, and
  **6 teaching modules** built from official data snapshots.
