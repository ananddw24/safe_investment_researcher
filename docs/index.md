# FinancialLibrary

**FinancialLibrary** is an open educational hub that helps low-income households
and women understand money basics, regulated savings and investment *types* by
country, and trusted public resources — without opening accounts, selling
products, or giving personalized advice.

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.
