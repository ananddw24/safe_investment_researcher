# Audience

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


Primary audiences:

- **Low-income households** seeking clear literacy materials
- **Women** navigating access, documentation, and autonomy barriers
- **Educators and NGOs** who need cite-first, fork-friendly content

We prioritize accessibility, liquidity, fees, and documentation barriers —
not “optimize alpha” framing.
