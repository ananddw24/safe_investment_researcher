# Using this library

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

FinancialLibrary is a **static reference**: short guides, country pages on
regulated savings and investment *types*, curated links to official sources,
and reading paths. It does not know your income, goals, or jurisdiction beyond
what you choose to read.

## How content is organized

1. **[Guides](../guides/index.md)** — concepts that apply in many places (emergency
   savings, inflation literacy, scam red flags).
2. **[Vehicles](../vehicles/index.md)** — plain-language pages on *types* of
   products households may encounter (bank deposits, pensions, mobile money, and
   similar), grouped by country.
3. **[Resources](../resources/index.md)** — outbound links to regulators,
   treasuries, deposit insurers, and similar **official** sites, stored as
   validated YAML and shown in the [resource catalog](../resources/catalog.md).
4. **[Pathways](../pathways/index.md)** — suggested reading orders; they never
   recommend a purchase or amount.

## Depth: “deep” vs “short” country pages

Not every country section has the same level of narrative detail yet. The
[Vehicles index](../vehicles/index.md) marks which releases added **deep** pages
(India, United States, Kenya) versus **short** sketches (many Top 10 GDP and
Africa cluster countries).

Short pages still follow the same rules: no product picks, no personalized
advice. Use them as **orientation**, then confirm details on the official sites
listed for your country in the [resource catalog](../resources/catalog.md).

## Rules we ask you to keep in mind

- **Verify on official sources.** Rates, limits, and eligibility change.
- **No guarantees.** Anyone promising safe, very high returns with little
  risk deserves skepticism — see
  [Spotting unregulated schemes](../guides/spotting-unregulated-schemes.md).
- **We do not open accounts.** This site does not collect financial data or
  sell products.
