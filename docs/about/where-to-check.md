# Where to check official figures

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

!!! danger "Not a forecast or a recommendation"
    This page does **not** publish rates, inflation percentages, or “what to
    buy.” It only points to **official publishers** so you can read current
    figures yourself. Numbers change. Always open the source.

**As of:** 2026-10-24 (page date; re-check links on the official sites below).

## How to use this page

1. Open your country’s row.
2. Visit the official site for inflation statistics or central-bank consumer
   education.
3. Read the figure on that site. Do not trust a screenshot from a tipster.
4. Pair the figure with [Inflation and capital preservation](../guides/inflation-capital-preservation.md)
   and [Emergency savings](../guides/emergency-savings.md) — literacy, not a
   product pick.

For curated link tables, use the [resource catalog](../resources/catalog.md).

## Top 10 economies

| Country | Inflation / statistics publisher | Banking / deposit context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| India | [Ministry of Statistics (MoSPI)](https://www.mospi.gov.in/) | [RBI Financial Education](https://www.rbi.org.in/FinancialEducation/) · [DICGC](https://www.dicgc.org.in/) |
| United States | Prefer Bureau of Labor Statistics / Fed education via [Federal Reserve consumers](https://www.federalreserve.gov/consumerscommunities.htm) | [FDIC deposit insurance](https://www.fdic.gov/resources/deposit-insurance/) · [CFPB](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/) |
| China | Prefer National Bureau of Statistics materials via ministry portals; start at [PBOC](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/) | [PBOC](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/) · [NFRA](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/) |
| Germany | Prefer Destatis / Eurostat via [Bundesbank](https://www.bundesbank.de/en) education pages | [Bundesbank](https://www.bundesbank.de/en) · [BaFin](https://www.bafin.de/EN/) |
| Japan | Prefer Statistics Bureau materials via [Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/) | [Bank of Japan](https://www.boj.or.jp/en/) · [JFSA](https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/) |
| United Kingdom | Prefer ONS materials; consumer protection via [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/) | [FSCS](https://www.fscs.org.uk/) · [NS&I](https://www.nsandi.com/) |
| France | Prefer INSEE materials via [Banque de France](https://www.banque-france.fr/en) | [Banque de France](https://www.banque-france.fr/en) · [service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.fr/) |
| Italy | Prefer Istat materials via [Banca d’Italia](https://www.bancaditalia.it/homepage/index.html?com.dotmarketing.htmlpage.language=1) | [Banca d’Italia](https://www.bancaditalia.it/homepage/index.html?com.dotmarketing.htmlpage.language=1) · [FITD](https://www.fitd.it/) |
| Brazil | Prefer IBGE materials via [Banco Central do Brasil](https://www.bcb.gov.br/en) | [BCB](https://www.bcb.gov.br/en) · [FGC](https://www.fgc.org.br/) |
| Canada | Prefer Statistics Canada materials via [Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/) | [Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/) · [CDIC](https://www.cdic.ca/) |
