# iDeCo concepts (Japan)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**iDeCo** (individual defined contribution pension) is a **personal pension account**
where participants choose contributions (within eligibility rules) and invest among
plan menus until retirement age, with **tax advantages** and **strict withdrawal
limits** before age 60 in ordinary cases.

It complements — does not replace — public pension and employer plans.

## Who it is typically designed for

Employees without generous employer DC plans, self-employed persons who qualify,
and others listed in official eligibility tables. **Not everyone can join** — verify
category on JFSA/MHLW materials before paying setup fees.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Very low** pre-retirement access except narrow hardship exceptions defined in law.
- Investment menus may include **principal-preserving** or **market-linked** options —
  read each fund’s risk class.

## Risks

- Contributing rent money because of tax-marketing pressure.
- **Fee-heavy** platform or fund choices within the plan menu.
- Stopping iDeCo while neglecting basic emergency cash.

## Literacy angle

Compare iDeCo vs [NISA](nisa.md): pension lock-up vs taxable-account flexibility.
Use official iDeCo pamphlets from JFSA before signing at a securities firm or bank.

## Official source types

- JFSA iDeCo and pension consumer information
- Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare pension context (via official portals)
