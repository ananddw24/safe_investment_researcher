# iDeCo concepts (Japan)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

iDeCo is a personal defined-contribution pension concept with long lock-up characteristics.

## Who it is typically designed for

Workers building long-term retirement savings who understand restricted access.

## Liquidity and commitments

Low — long horizon by design.

## Risks

Market-linked options; contribution gaps; early access limits.

## Literacy angle

Verify current rules on official JFSA / pension materials before informal tips.
