# Ordinary bank and Japan Post Bank deposits

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Ordinary deposits at banks and Japan Post Bank are the default household savings channel.

## Who it is typically designed for

Most households needing payments and a cash buffer.

## Liquidity and commitments

Typically high for ordinary deposits.

## Risks

Fees, fraud, and product mix-ups at counters.

## Literacy angle

Confirm deposit vs investment labeling in writing.

## Official source types

JFSA; Bank of Japan education; Japan Post Bank.
