# Stokvels — informal rotating savings (South Africa)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Stokvels are cultural rotating savings groups. They can build discipline — and can fail through theft or weak rules.

## Who it is typically designed for

Community groups, including many women-led stokvels — literacy on documentation and trust.

## Liquidity and commitments

Schedule-driven; missed contributions create social and financial pressure.

## Risks

Theft, poor record-keeping, and pressure to borrow.

## Literacy angle

Written rules, dual control of funds, and FSCA consumer tips on informal schemes.

## Official source types

FSCA consumer education.
