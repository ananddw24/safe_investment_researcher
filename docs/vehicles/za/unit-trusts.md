# Money market and unit trusts / CIS (South Africa)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Collective investment schemes (unit trusts), including money market funds, are FSCA-regulated investments; values can move.

## Who it is typically designed for

People with emergency cash who check fees and fund fact sheets.

## Liquidity and commitments

Fund-specific; not a deposit guarantee product.

## Risks

Market/credit risk; fee drag; unlicensed schemes.

## Literacy angle

Verify FSCA-regulated status before contributing.

## Official source types

FSCA.
