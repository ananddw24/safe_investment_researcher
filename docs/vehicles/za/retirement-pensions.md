# Retirement annuity and pension fund basics (South Africa)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Retirement annuities and pension funds are long-term vehicles under South African rules (FSCA / tax authority materials).

## Who it is typically designed for

Workers planning long horizons; informal workers may lack coverage.

## Liquidity and commitments

Low — restricted access by design.

## Risks

Market-linked options; contribution gaps; hard-sell products.

## Literacy angle

Verify on FSCA and SARS pages before informal tips.

## Official source types

FSCA; SARS.
