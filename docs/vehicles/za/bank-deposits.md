# Bank savings and fixed deposits (South Africa)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Bank savings and fixed deposits are foundational household channels. Consumer education is associated with SARB and FSCA materials.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households building emergency cash; watch fees on small balances.

## Liquidity and commitments

Savings: higher access. Fixed deposits: term tradeoffs.

## Risks

Fees, fraud, and inflation erosion of purchasing power.

## Literacy angle

Emergency cash first; read bank fee guides and regulator consumer pages.

## Official source types

SARB; FSCA.
