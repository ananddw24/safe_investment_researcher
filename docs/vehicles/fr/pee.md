# PEE (Plan d'épargne entreprise) concepts

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

The **PEE** is an **employer-sponsored collective savings plan** where employees
(and often employers) contribute into locked compartments with fund menus until
events defined in the plan rules (departure, retirement, etc.). **PEE ≠ Livret A**
and is not guaranteed cash.

## Who it is typically designed for

Employees whose companies offer a PEE. Part-time and CDD workers should verify access
and **matching (abondement)** formulas in the **règlement du plan**.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Blocked** except for statutory **cases of déblocage** listed in official guidance
  and your plan rulebook.
- Fund units can fall in value on UCITS options.

## Risks

- Contributing without capturing full **employer match** because of payroll timing errors.
- Keeping old PEE accounts at former employers without updating coordinates.
- Confusing PEE with **PER** retirement products.

## Literacy angle

Collect HR’s **booklet réglementaire** and AMF fund factsheets. Maintain
[Livret-style liquidity](livret-a-ldds.md) for shocks.
