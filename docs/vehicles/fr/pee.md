# Employer savings / PEE concepts (France)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

PEE and related employer savings plans are employer-linked frameworks under French rules.

## Who it is typically designed for

Employees whose employers offer a plan.

## Liquidity and commitments

Often restricted; exceptions are rule-based.

## Risks

Misunderstanding lock-ups; investment options inside the plan can fall.

## Literacy angle

Ask for official plan documents; verify on public service materials.

## Official source types

service-public.fr; AMF for investment literacy.
