# Livret A and LDDS (France)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Livret A** and **LDDS** (Livret de Développement Durable et Solidaire) are
regulated **passbook savings** products with interest formulas and **deposit ceilings**
set by public authorities. They are widely used for accessible household cash and
are distinct from stock market or **assurance-vie** contracts.

Rates and caps change by decree — verify on [service-public.fr](https://www.service-public.fr/)
and Banque de France notices, not forwarded WhatsApp tables.

## Who it is typically designed for

Residents meeting eligibility rules (Livret A is broadly available; LDDS has specific
conditions). Families often open **Livret A** for minors — understand who legally
controls withdrawals.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **High liquidity** within regulatory rules; transfers between authorized institutions
  follow published processes.
- **Ceilings:** once capped, excess must move elsewhere — plan before automatic blocks.

## Risks

- Assuming historic rates continue during inflation shifts.
- Confusing Livret products with **PEL** housing savings or market investments.
- Fraud targeting “Livret A bonus” phishing.

## Literacy angle

Check current **taux**, **plafond**, and tax treatment on official pages before
year-end deposits. Pair with [LEP](lep.md) literacy if income eligibility applies.
