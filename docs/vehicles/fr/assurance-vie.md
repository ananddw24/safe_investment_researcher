# Assurance-vie basics (France)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Assurance-vie** is an **insurance wrapper** commonly used for long-term saving and
estate planning. Contracts may hold **euro funds (fonds en euros)** with insurer-
published guarantees (subject to insurer solvency rules) and/or **unit-linked (UCITS)**
funds with market risk. It is **not** a Livret deposit.

Complex fee structures (entry, management, arbitrage) appear in **DIC** documents.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households with emergency cash and long horizons who will read contract fees and
understand **rachat** (withdrawal) tax effects. Hard-sell at banks and doorsteps
is a known consumer issue — take documents home before signing.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Partial withdrawals** possible but may trigger tax and loss of bonus guarantees on
  some euro funds.
- **Unit-linked** portions can lose value quickly in downturns.

## Risks

- **Fee drag** and opaque **frais d'entrée**.
- Switching funds based on banker pressure without reading **KIID**.
- Scams mimicking assurance-vie transfers.

## Literacy angle

AMF investor education explains UCITS risk; ACPR/Banque de France cover insurance
conduct. Compare at least two **DIC/IPID** documents before committing.

## Official source types

- Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF)
- Banque de France / ACPR consumer protection

Links: [AMF EN](https://www.amf-france.org/en),
