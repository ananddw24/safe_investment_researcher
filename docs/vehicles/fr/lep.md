# LEP (Livret d'épargne populaire)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

The **LEP** is a regulated savings booklet for households meeting **income ceilings**
published in official texts. It often pays a higher regulated rate than Livret A,
but **eligibility must be re-proven** periodically with documentation.

## Who it is typically designed for

Lower- and moderate-income households who qualify under current income plafonds.
If income rises above limits, rules may require closure or transfer — follow bank
and service-public guidance.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Accessible savings within LEP rules and caps.
- **Proof of eligibility** is part of the product — keep tax notices or official
  income documents organized.

## Risks

- Opening without valid eligibility, then forced correction and tax questions.
- Rumors that LEP is “unlimited high interest for everyone.”

## Literacy angle

Verify **plafonds de ressources** each year on service-public.fr before depositing
large sums. Combine with [Livret A / LDDS](livret-a-ldds.md) planning within caps.
