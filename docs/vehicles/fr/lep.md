# Livret d’épargne populaire / LEP (France)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

LEP is a means-tested regulated savings product — highly relevant for lower-income literacy.

## Who it is typically designed for

Eligible lower-income residents under published income criteria.

## Liquidity and commitments

Subject to official product rules and caps.

## Risks

Eligibility mistakes; unofficial “LEP helpers” collecting fees.

## Literacy angle

Confirm eligibility on service-public.fr.
