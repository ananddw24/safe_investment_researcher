# Gold: Sovereign Gold Bonds vs physical

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Many Indian households hold **physical gold** (jewelry or coins) as a cultural
store of value. **Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGBs)** are government-issued securities
denominated in gold, offered in issuance windows under published terms (interest,
tenure, redemption). Jewelry is primarily adornment; making charges and purity
issues make it a poor “investment lesson” if treated like a financial product.

## Who it is typically designed for

- Physical gold: cultural and consumption use; storage and purity literacy matter.
- SGBs: people comfortable with demat/banking channels and issuance schedules —
  not everyone will find a live tranche when they look.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Physical: sale depends on local markets; distress sales often fetch less than
  expected after making charges.
- SGB: secondary liquidity and exit rules follow the specific bond terms and market
  conditions — read the official term sheet for each issuance.

## Risks

- Physical: theft, impurity, storage, and high buy-sell spreads.
- SGB: gold price can fall; issuance may be closed; understand interest and capital
  components from official docs only.
- Unregulated “gold investment schemes” promising fixed high returns are a classic
  scam pattern.

## Literacy angle

Teach **jewelry ≠ investment**, opportunity cost, and how to verify whether a gold
product is a regulated instrument or an informal scheme.

## Official source types

- RBI / government notifications for SGB issuances
- SEBI investor education for scam patterns
- BIS / quality marks literacy for physical gold (hallmarking)

See [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/), [SEBI Investor Portal](https://investor.sebi.gov.in/),
[myScheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/).
