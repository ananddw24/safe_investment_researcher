# National Pension System (NPS)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

The National Pension System is a **voluntary, regulated retirement-savings
framework** overseen in India with recordkeeping and fund management under
published PFRDA rules. Subscribers choose asset mixes within allowed options;
outcomes depend on contributions, costs, and market-linked components.

## Who it is typically designed for

Workers and self-employed people building long-term retirement savings who can
tolerate limited access to money before exit rules allow. Informal workers should
weigh irregular income against auto-debit commitments.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Liquidity:** low — Tier structures and exit/partial-withdrawal rules are
  specific; “Tier” literacy matters before any contribution.
- Annuity / lump-sum options at exit are rule-based — learn the vocabulary from
  PFRDA/NPS Trust materials, not from tip videos.

## Risks

- Market-linked portions can fall in value.
- Early exit and tax treatment are complex and change — verify officially.
- Fees and contribution gaps both affect outcomes for small savers.

## Literacy angle

NPS teaches **retirement horizon**, diversification *as a concept*, and the
difference between pension products and short-term deposits.

## Official source types

- Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA)
- NPS Trust
- SEBI investor education for adjacent market literacy

Start at [PFRDA](https://www.pfrda.org.in/) and [NPS Trust](https://npstrust.org.in/).
