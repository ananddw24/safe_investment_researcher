# Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a **girl-child focused** small-savings scheme under
the government small-savings framework. Deposits are made in the name of an
eligible girl child by a guardian, subject to age, account limits, and deposit
windows published in official rules.

## Who it is typically designed for

Caregivers planning long-term education or marriage-related costs *as the scheme
documents describe* — not as a personalized plan. Documentation (birth proof,
guardian ID) is part of access.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Liquidity:** low — long deposit/withdrawal structure with conditions.
- Deposit limits and tenure are rule-bound; missing the deposit window can affect
  the account status under published norms.

## Risks

- Eligibility mistakes (age, number of accounts) can block opening or create
  compliance issues.
- Long lock-in is unsuitable for near-term emergency needs.
- Scheme parameters (interest, limits) are notified and can change.

## Literacy angle

SSY is a strong teaching case for **purpose-labeled saving**, guardian
responsibilities, and reading eligibility before collecting informal “tips.”

## Official source types

- DFS / Ministry of Finance small-savings materials
- India Post and agency banks
- myScheme / official scheme aggregators for summaries (always confirm primary text)

See [Financial Services](https://financialservices.gov.in/),
[India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/), [myScheme](https://www.myscheme.gov.in/).
