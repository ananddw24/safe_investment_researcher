# India Post savings schemes

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

India Post offers a range of **savings and small-savings products** (savings
accounts, recurring/time deposits, and agency roles for certain government schemes)
through the postal network. Exact products and rates are published by India Post /
small-savings authorities and change over time.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households that trust or can physically reach a post office — often important in
rural and semi-urban areas. Useful when bank branches are far, but still requires
KYC and careful reading of each product’s rules.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Varies by product** — from relatively accessible savings accounts to term
  deposits and long small-savings schemes.
- Do not assume postal products are identical to bank products with similar names.

## Risks

- Product confusion (deposit vs scheme vs insurance cross-sell at counters).
- Travel/time costs if the post office is far; digital alternatives may or may not
  be available for a given product.
- Fraudsters impersonating postal staff — verify through official channels.

## Literacy angle

Postal savings highlight **access geography** and the habit of asking for the
written product leaflet, not only oral explanations at the counter.

## Official source types

- India Post official site
- DFS / small-savings notifications
- RBI education for general banking safety habits

See [India Post](https://www.indiapost.gov.in/) and
[Financial Services](https://financialservices.gov.in/).
