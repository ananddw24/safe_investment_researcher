# Fixed Deposit (FD)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

A Fixed Deposit parks a lump sum with a bank (or certain postal products) for a
stated term at a stated interest schedule. It is a deposit product, not a market
investment.

## Who it is typically designed for

People with a **known surplus** they will not need during the term. Senior-citizen
rate differences, if any, are set by the issuer — always read the current sheet.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Liquidity:** low to medium — early withdrawal usually reduces interest and may
  add penalties.
- “Laddering” (splitting across maturities) is a *teaching idea* about liquidity
  planning, not a recommendation to open multiple products.

## Risks

- Reinvestment risk when the term ends and rates differ.
- Breaking an FD for an emergency can be costly — fund emergencies elsewhere first.
- Deposit insurance literacy (DICGC) covers eligible bank deposits up to published
  limits — understand what is and is not covered.

## Literacy angle

FDs illustrate **time vs access**. Compare the term to your cash-flow calendar and
to inflation, not to social-media “highest rate” lists.

## Official source types

- Bank FD disclosures
- DICGC deposit insurance explainers
- RBI financial education

See [DICGC](https://www.dicgc.org.in/) and [RBI Financial Education](https://www.rbi.org.in/FinancialEducation/).
