# Poupança (Brazil)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Caderneta de poupança** is a regulated savings account type whose **remuneration
rules** are set in law and linked to benchmark rates (commonly described in relation
to **TR** and Selic thresholds in official explainers). It is widely used as a first
formal savings product but is **not immune to inflation** in real terms.

## Who it is typically designed for

Broad households, including Nubank/branch users offered poupança as default savings.
Compare whether **RDB/CDB** or other products fit your liquidity needs — literacy only,
not a recommendation either way.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Withdrawals follow calendar rules (including the famous **aniversário** crediting
  logic) — read BCB consumer materials for current mechanics.
- Relatively accessible compared with long **CDB** lock-ins.

## Risks

- **Inflation erosion** of purchasing power during high IPCA periods.
- Fraud via Pix social engineering.
- Keeping all wealth in poupança while ignoring **FGC** limits on other deposit products
  you may hold elsewhere.

## Literacy angle

Use [BCB](https://www.bcb.gov.br/) education on poupança rules and compare **real**
returns using official inflation statistics literacy — not influencer “Selic arbitrage.”

## Official source types

- Banco Central do Brasil (BCB)
- FGC deposit guarantee for eligible products (context for other accounts)
