# Susu and cooperative collectors (Ghana)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Susu and cooperative collectors are informal or semi-formal savings practices with high community trust — and high operational risk.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households, often women, using collector-based discipline.

## Liquidity and commitments

Schedule-driven; missing collections creates pressure.

## Risks

Collector flight, weak records, and coercive borrowing.

## Literacy angle

Written records, dual control, and preferring licensed channels when possible — BoG inclusion materials.

## Official source types

Bank of Ghana financial inclusion materials.
