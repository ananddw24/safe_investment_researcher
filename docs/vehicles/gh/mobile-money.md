# Mobile money wallets and interest-bearing features (Ghana)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Mobile money wallets are a major inclusion channel. Some features look like savings; payment-system rules and fees matter.

## Who it is typically designed for

People who transact primarily by phone, including low-income and rural users.

## Liquidity and commitments

Typically high for wallet balances; interest-bearing or lock features have published terms.

## Risks

Fraud, PIN sharing, and unregulated investment apps attached to mobile money brands.

## Literacy angle

Read tariffs; separate float from licensed deposits.

## Official source types

Bank of Ghana payment rules / consumer materials.
