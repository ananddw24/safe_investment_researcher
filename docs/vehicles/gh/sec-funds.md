# SEC-licensed money market and mutual funds (Ghana)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Money market and mutual funds offered to the public should be SEC Ghana–licensed. Values can move.

## Who it is typically designed for

People who will verify licensing before contributing.

## Liquidity and commitments

Fund-specific; not a bank deposit.

## Risks

Unlicensed funds; “guaranteed returns”; fee drag.

## Literacy angle

Licensing checks before money moves.

## Official source types

SEC Ghana; Bank of Ghana context.

Links: [SEC Ghana](https://sec.gov.gh/), [Bank of Ghana](https://www.bog.gov.gh/).
