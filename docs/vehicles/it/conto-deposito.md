# Conto deposito / bank savings (Italy)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Conto corrente** (current account) and **conto deposito** / **libretto di risparmio**
products at Italian banks hold cash for payments and short-term saving. **Vincoli** (time
ties) may apply higher rates but restrict access. These are banking products governed
by deposit rules and **FITD** (Fondo Interbancario di Tutela dei Depositi) protection
within legal limits.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households using formal banking for salaries, pensions, and bill payment. Compare
**canone** (account fees), card costs, and bundled insurance you did not request.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Sight deposits:** generally high access within operational limits.
- **Time-linked deposits:** penalties for early break per contract.

## Risks

- **Phishing** and PSD2 payment fraud.
- Confusing **conto deposito** with **fondi comuni** sold at the same desk.
- Fees eroding small balances.

## Literacy angle

Read FITD and Banca d’Italia leaflets on **deposit guarantee** before assuming “all
money is safe regardless of amount.” Build emergency cash in flexible accounts before
[BTP](btp-retail.md) or [funds](fondi-pir.md).
