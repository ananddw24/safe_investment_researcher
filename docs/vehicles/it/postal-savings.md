# Postal savings (Italy)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Poste Italiane** savings products (including **Libretto di risparmio postale** and
related offerings) combine postal network access with products governed by specific
public rules and disclosures. They are widely used in rural areas and among savers
who prefer counter service — but product type still determines risk (deposit vs investment).

## Who it is typically designed for

Households with limited branch banking nearby. Seniors and caregivers should clarify
**account holder identity** and appointment of attorneys on official forms.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Varies by product generation — read the **foglio informativo** for withdrawal rules.
- Some products tie bonuses to holding periods.

## Risks

- Counter **cross-selling** of investment products when you asked for simple savings.
- Scams using fake Poste SMS links.

## Literacy angle

Verify any “special postal rate” on Poste and Banca d’Italia linked official pages,
not chain messages.

## Official source types

- Poste Italiane product sheets
- Banca d’Italia / Ministry publications on postal savings frameworks

Links: [Poste Italiane](https://www.poste.it/), [Banca d’Italia](https://www.bancaditalia.it/).
