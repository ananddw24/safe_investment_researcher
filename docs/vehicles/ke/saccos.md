# SACCOs (savings and credit cooperatives)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Savings and Credit Cooperative Organizations (**SACCOs**) pool member savings and
often provide credit. In Kenya, deposit-taking SACCOs are associated with
**SASRA** oversight. Governance quality varies — literacy means reading bylaws,
understanding withdrawal rules, and verifying regulatory status.

## Who it is typically designed for

Workers and community groups seeking cooperative savings discipline. Women often
participate through workplace or community SACCOs — group pressure can help or harm.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Share deposits may have notice periods.
- Loans create repayment obligations; multiple overlapping loans are a risk pattern.

## Risks

- Poor governance or fraud at the society level.
- Confusing shares with insured bank deposits.
- Coercive recovery practices.

## Literacy angle

Ask for SASRA status (where applicable), audited accounts, and written withdrawal
rules before large contributions.
