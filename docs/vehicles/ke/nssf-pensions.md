# NSSF and pension basics

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

The **National Social Security Fund (NSSF)** and broader retirement-benefits
frameworks provide formal-worker pension literacy in Kenya. Contribution rules,
benefits, and residual benefits authority materials change — verify on official
NSSF pages. The Retirement Benefits Authority (RBA) is the named pensions
regulator; confirm details on official RBA channels when accessible.

## Who it is typically designed for

Formal employees and others covered under published NSSF rules. Informal workers
may lack coverage — literacy includes knowing what is *not* automatically provided.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Pension savings are long-horizon with restricted access under scheme rules.
- Not a substitute for short-term emergency cash.

## Risks

- Informal “pension clubs” with no legal standing.
- Incomplete contribution histories.
- Misunderstanding benefits eligibility.

## Literacy angle

Use NSSF’s official materials to learn contribution and benefit vocabulary; treat
unsolicited pension investment tips with the same skepticism as other schemes.

## Official source types

- NSSF Kenya
- Retirement Benefits Authority (verify on official RBA channels)
- CBK financial education (adjacent)
