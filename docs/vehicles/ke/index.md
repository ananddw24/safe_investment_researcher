# Kenya — vehicles

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


These pages explain **accessible** savings and investment *types* for ordinary
Kenyan households, with attention to mobile money inclusion, cooperative savings,
and licensing. Literacy only — not product picks. Prefer regulated or supervised
channels and verify on official CBK, CMA, SASRA, Treasury, and NSSF materials.
