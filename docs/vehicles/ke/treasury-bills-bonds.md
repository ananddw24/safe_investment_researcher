# Treasury bills and bonds

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

The Government of Kenya issues **Treasury bills and bonds**. Retail access channels
and historical products (including retail bond initiatives discussed in public
education) change over time. Literacy focuses on government debt mechanics,
holding periods, and inflation — not tip-driven “sure rates.”

## Who it is typically designed for

Savers who already understand emergency liquidity and can follow official auction
or retail subscription instructions from the National Treasury / CBK materials.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Bills and bonds have stated tenors; early exit depends on market and product rules.
- Keep emergency money separate.

## Risks

- Interest-rate and inflation can affect real outcomes and secondary prices.
- Fake “government bond” collectors.
- FX and macro stress are context for capital-preservation literacy, not trade ideas.

## Literacy angle

Use National Treasury and CBK publications to learn how issuance works and how to
spot impersonation.

## Official source types

- National Treasury of Kenya
- Central Bank of Kenya
