# Bank and deposit-taking microfinance deposits

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Commercial banks and some **deposit-taking** microfinance institutions offer
savings and fixed deposits under Central Bank of Kenya licensing. Credit-only
lenders are different — they may not take regulated deposits. Literacy means
checking what kind of institution you are dealing with.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households that can reach a branch or agent banking channel and need a formal
deposit account. Women and low-income savers should compare fees and documentation
requirements.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Savings: typically higher access; fixed deposits: term commitments and break
  penalties may apply.
- Always read the institution’s published terms.

## Risks

- Dealing with an unlicensed collector labeled as a “bank.”
- Fees and dormant-account rules.
- Fraud via fake agents.

## Literacy angle

Ask: Is this CBK-licensed? Is this deposit-taking or credit-only? Where is the
written fee schedule?
