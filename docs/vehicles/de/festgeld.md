# Festgeld (Germany)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Festgeld is a fixed-term bank deposit with agreed tenure and interest terms.

## Who it is typically designed for

People with known surplus they will not need during the term.

## Liquidity and commitments

Low–medium; early break rules may apply.

## Risks

Reinvestment risk; locking emergency money.

## Literacy angle

Match tenure to dated goals after emergency cash exists.
