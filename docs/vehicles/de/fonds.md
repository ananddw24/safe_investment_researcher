# Regulated mutual funds / Fonds (Germany)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Regulated mutual funds (Fonds) sold via banks or platforms are market investments; prices can fall.

## Who it is typically designed for

People with emergency savings who will check costs.

## Liquidity and commitments

Usually redeemable on fund rules; not a deposit.

## Risks

Market loss; fee drag; unsuitable short horizons.

## Literacy angle

Read cost and risk disclosures from BaFin investor education.

## Official source types

BaFin.
