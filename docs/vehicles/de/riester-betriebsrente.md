# Riester and Betriebsrente concepts (Germany)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Riester and workplace pension (**Betriebsrente**) concepts are long-term retirement frameworks with eligibility complexity.

## Who it is typically designed for

Workers navigating employer and state-supported pension options — literacy first.

## Liquidity and commitments

Low; long horizon and scheme rules.

## Risks

Misunderstanding subsidies/eligibility; high-cost products sold as “must have.”

## Literacy angle

Use Deutsche Rentenversicherung and BaFin materials — not tip sellers.

## Official source types

Deutsche Rentenversicherung; BaFin.
