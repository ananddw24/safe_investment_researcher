# Stocks & Shares ISA / funds basics (UK)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

A Stocks & Shares ISA holds investments inside an ISA wrapper; values can fall.

## Who it is typically designed for

People with emergency cash who accept market risk.

## Liquidity and commitments

Investments can be sold subject to market/provider rules; prices move.

## Risks

Market loss; fees; scam “guaranteed ISA returns.”

## Literacy angle

Use FCA investor materials and GOV.UK ISA pages.

## Official source types

FCA; GOV.UK.

Links: [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/), [ISAs](https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts).
