# Stocks & Shares ISA / funds basics (UK)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

A **Stocks & Shares ISA** is a tax wrapper holding **investments** (funds, shares,
bonds) at an authorised provider. Value rises and falls with markets. It is **not**
deposit insurance territory — different risks from [easy-access savings](easy-access-savings.md).

## Who it is typically designed for

Households with emergency cash and a multi-year horizon who will read **KIID/KID**
fund documents and tolerate drawdowns. Not suitable for rent money or short deadlines.

## Liquidity and commitments

- You can usually **sell** holdings, but settlement takes days and proceeds may be
  **lower** than invested.
- **ISA allowance** is consumed when you subscribe — plan subscriptions using GOV.UK rules.

## Risks

- **Capital loss**, especially concentrated single-stock bets promoted online.
- **Platform and fund fees** compounding over time.
- Scams promising “guaranteed ISA returns” or unregulated crypto inside ISA marketing.

## Literacy angle

FCA’s **ScamSmart** and investor education pages explain warning signs. Start with
diversified fund literacy before single-stock speculation.

## Official source types

- FCA investor guidance
- GOV.UK ISA rules
