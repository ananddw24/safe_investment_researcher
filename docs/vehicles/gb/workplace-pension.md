# Workplace pension / auto-enrolment (UK)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Workplace pensions** with **automatic enrolment** require eligible employers to
enrol qualifying workers into a pension scheme and contribute minimum amounts under
rules overseen by **The Pensions Regulator (TPR)**. Most members save through **defined
contribution** pots invested in funds until retirement age — values can fall.

You may **opt out**, but literacy means understanding long-term effects before doing so.

## Who it is typically designed for

Employees aged 22+ earning above the earnings trigger (verify current thresholds on
GOV.UK). Zero-hour and multiple-job workers should check eligibility per job.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Locked for decades** except narrow circumstances (severe ill health, etc.) under
  pension law — not an emergency fund.
- **Employer matching** is often the main reason to stay enrolled — learn your scheme’s
  formula from the official booklet, not cafetería gossip.

## Risks

- **Opting out** to boost take-home pay without replacing long-term savings elsewhere.
- High-charging or poorly explained **default funds**.
- Pension scams offering “free pension reviews” and transfers to unregulated schemes.

## Literacy angle

Use [TPR](https://www.thepensionsregulator.gov.uk/) and [GOV.UK workplace pensions](https://www.gov.uk/workplace-pensions)
before changing contributions. Keep [easy-access cash](easy-access-savings.md) separate.

## Official source types

- The Pensions Regulator
- GOV.UK and MoneyHelper pension guidance
