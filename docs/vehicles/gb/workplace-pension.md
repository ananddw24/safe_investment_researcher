# Workplace pension / auto-enrolment (UK)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Workplace pensions with auto-enrolment are a default long-term path for many workers under The Pensions Regulator framework.

## Who it is typically designed for

Employees; opt-out and contribution rules are published officially.

## Liquidity and commitments

Low — retirement horizon.

## Risks

Opting out without understanding long-term effects; high-cost funds.

## Literacy angle

Use TPR and GOV.UK workplace pension pages.

## Official source types

The Pensions Regulator; GOV.UK.
