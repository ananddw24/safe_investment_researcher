# Premium Bonds (NS&I)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Premium Bonds** from National Savings & Investments (NS&I) are a **prize-linked**
product backed by HM Treasury in the sense described on NS&I’s site. You do not
earn a guaranteed interest rate; instead you receive **prize draws** with odds and
prize rates published by NS&I. Capital is repayable per NS&I terms when you cash in.

## Who it is typically designed for

Savers who understand **variable, uncertain returns** and treat prizes as a lottery-
like feature, not income planning. Poor substitute for emergency cash if you rely on
predictable interest to cover bills.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Cash-in requests follow NS&I processing times — not always instant for large holdings.
- **Maximum holding limits** apply — check NS&I current rules.

## Risks

- **Prize variance:** you may win nothing for long periods despite holding bonds.
- **Opportunity cost** vs interest-bearing easy-access accounts in high-rate periods.
- Scams impersonating NS&I asking for remote access or “bond activation fees.”

## Literacy angle

Read NS&I’s published **annual prize fund rate** and odds statements honestly — avoid
influencer “systems” claiming guaranteed wins.
