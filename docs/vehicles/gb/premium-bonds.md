# Premium Bonds (NS&I)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

NS&I Premium Bonds are a prize-based product; capital treatment follows NS&I published terms — explain odds honestly.

## Who it is typically designed for

People who understand prizes are not a fixed interest rate.

## Liquidity and commitments

Subject to NS&I holding and repayment rules.

## Risks

Prize variance; opportunity cost vs interest-bearing cash.

## Literacy angle

Read NS&I official pages — not influencer “systems.”
