# Easy-access and notice savings (UK)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Easy-access and notice savings accounts hold emergency cash at banks/building societies under UK rules.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households needing a liquid buffer; watch fees and FSCS eligibility literacy.

## Liquidity and commitments

Easy-access: high; notice accounts: delayed access.

## Risks

Fees; fraud; confusing savings with investments.

## Literacy angle

Use FCA and FSCS materials for protection literacy.

## Official source types

FCA; FSCS; Bank of England context.
