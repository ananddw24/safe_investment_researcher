# Cash ISA concepts (UK)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

A Cash ISA is a tax wrapper for cash savings with annual allowance rules published by HMRC/GOV.UK.

## Who it is typically designed for

Savers learning tax wrappers after emergency needs are understood.

## Liquidity and commitments

Usually accessible cash, subject to provider rules and ISA transfer rules.

## Risks

Misunderstanding allowances; provider fees.

## Literacy angle

Read GOV.UK ISA pages for current allowance facts.

## Official source types

HMRC / GOV.UK; FCA.

Links: [ISAs on GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/individual-savings-accounts), [HMRC](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-revenue-customs), [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/).
