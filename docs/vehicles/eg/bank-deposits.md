# Bank savings and certificates of deposit (Egypt)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Bank savings and certificates of deposit are a dominant household channel under Central Bank of Egypt (CBE) framing.

## Who it is typically designed for

Broad public needing formal savings; documentation and fees vary.

## Liquidity and commitments

Sight savings vs certificates differ sharply in access.

## Risks

Fees; fraud; inflation vs nominal rate literacy; product mix-ups.

## Literacy angle

Emergency accessible cash before long certificates.

## Official source types

CBE.
