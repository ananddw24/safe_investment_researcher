# Microfinance and NGO savings groups (Egypt)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Microfinance and NGO savings groups can expand women’s access — with debt caution. FRA microfinance rules are a literacy anchor where applicable.

## Who it is typically designed for

Women and low-income households using group savings/credit.

## Liquidity and commitments

Savings schedules and loan repayments create obligations.

## Risks

Over-indebtedness; coercive collection; unlicensed lenders.

## Literacy angle

Separate saving from borrowing; verify FRA-related licensing where relevant.

## Official source types

FRA; CBE.
