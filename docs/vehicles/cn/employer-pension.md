# Employer pension / enterprise annuity concepts (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

China’s pension system combines **public pillars** with **employer-sponsored**
arrangements such as enterprise annuities and occupational plans where employers
choose to participate. These are **long-term, restricted-access** savings
vehicles governed by pension and tax rules — not short-term cash jars.

This page is concept literacy only; plan names, contribution caps, and investment
menus change by regulation and employer.

## Who it is typically designed for

Formal employees whose employers sponsor a plan. Gig, informal, and migrant
workers may have **partial or no access** to the same channels — coverage gaps
are a policy and household-planning reality, not a personal failure.

Women and secondary earners should understand **whose name** holds the account,
what happens on job change, and how survivor benefits are documented in official
materials.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Contributions are typically **locked for retirement** with limited early-access
  exceptions defined in law and plan rules.
- **Portability** on employer change depends on plan type and current policy —
  verify on official portals before relying on informal HR summaries.
- Do not fund essential monthly expenses from pension balances you cannot access.

## Risks

- Treating informal “pension clubs” or unregistered pools as equivalent to regulated
  employer plans.
- **Incomplete documentation** of vesting (when employer contributions become yours).
- Confusing **commercial insurance** sales at the workplace with mandatory pension
  contributions.

## Literacy angle

Learn the vocabulary — basic pension, enterprise annuity, individual pension
account (where applicable) — from government and regulator pages **before**
product pitches. Build [emergency savings](../../guides/emergency-savings.md)
outside pension lock-ins.

## Official source types

- Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security materials (via gov.cn portals)
- PBOC and NFRA context on pension finance and consumer protection
