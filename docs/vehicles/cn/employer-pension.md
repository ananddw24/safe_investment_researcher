# Employer pension / enterprise annuity concepts (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Employer-linked pension or enterprise annuity concepts are long-term, restricted-access retirement savings under official pension rules.

## Who it is typically designed for

Formal workers whose employers offer a plan. Informal workers may have no access.

## Liquidity and commitments

Low — long horizon; early access rules are scheme-specific.

## Risks

Incomplete coverage; confusing informal “pension clubs” with regulated plans.

## Literacy angle

Learn contribution and vesting vocabulary from official materials before informal tips.

## Official source types

Official pension authority / government portals.
