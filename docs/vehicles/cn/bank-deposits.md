# Bank demand and time deposits (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Demand (current) and time deposits at banks are the core household savings channel. Early-withdrawal rules and rates are set by the bank under applicable regulation.

## Who it is typically designed for

Ordinary households needing a place for wages and short or timed savings. Documentation and branch access vary.

## Liquidity and commitments

**Demand:** high access. **Time deposits:** lower access; breaking early may reduce interest.

## Risks

Fees, fraud, and confusing deposit products with investment “wealth” products.

## Literacy angle

Read the deposit contract and ask whether principal is a deposit or an investment product.

## Official source types

People’s Bank of China (PBOC); banking/insurance regulator materials (NFRA and successors).
