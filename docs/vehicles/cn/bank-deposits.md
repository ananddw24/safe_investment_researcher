# Bank demand and time deposits (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Demand (current) deposits** and **time (fixed) deposits** at licensed banks are
the main regulated channel where many households hold wages, transfers, and short-
or medium-term savings. Demand accounts support day-to-day payments; time
deposits usually offer a stated term and may pay different interest if you hold
to maturity.

Deposits are not the same as bank **wealth management** or fund products sold at
the same counter — see [wealth vs deposits](wealth-vs-deposits.md).

## Who it is typically designed for

Households in the formal banking system who need a place for incoming money and a
predictable place for timed savings. Documentation (identity, mobile number,
address) and branch or app access vary by bank and product. Rural and migrant
households may face extra friction opening or maintaining accounts — verify current
inclusion guidance from official sources rather than informal agents.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Demand deposits:** generally high access for ordinary withdrawals and
  transfers, subject to bank rules, limits, and holds.
- **Time deposits:** lower access until maturity; **early withdrawal** often
  reduces or forfeits interest according to the contract.
- Read whether the product is **renewable automatically** at maturity and what
  rate applies on rollover.

## Risks

- **Product confusion:** signing a wealth or investment product while believing
  it is a deposit.
- **Fraud:** fake bank apps, phishing, and “customer service” calls asking for
  codes or remote-control software.
- **Fees and minimums** that erode small balances if you do not read the schedule.
- **Inflation literacy:** deposits prioritize capital stability and access, not
  beating inflation — see the [inflation guide](../../guides/inflation-capital-preservation.md).

## Literacy angle

Before you sign, ask in writing: Is this **principal guaranteed as a deposit**?
Which institution holds it? What happens if you need money before maturity?
Build emergency cash in **demand or very flexible** forms before locking longer
terms.

## Official source types

- People’s Bank of China (PBOC) consumer and financial-stability materials
- National Financial Regulatory Administration (NFRA) banking consumer information
- Your bank’s published product description and deposit agreement

Links: [PBOC](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/), [NFRA](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/),
[gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/).
