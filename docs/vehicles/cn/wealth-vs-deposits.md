# Postal / bank wealth products vs deposits (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Major banks and postal savings outlets may sell both **regulated deposits** and
**wealth management / investment-linked products** in the same branch or app.
Marketing language often emphasizes “stable” or “higher” returns. The literacy
task is to identify which category you are buying **before** you transfer money.

Deposits fall under banking/deposit rules; wealth products carry investment and
counterparty risks disclosed in separate documents.

## Who it is typically designed for

Any household using bank or postal counters — especially older savers and first-
time investors approached with “special rate” offers. Low-literacy environments
are where **mis-selling** harm is most common; ask for written classification.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Deposits:** maturity and early-withdrawal rules per
  [bank deposits](bank-deposits.md).
- **Wealth products:** may have lock periods, performance fees, and **principal
  at risk** depending on structure — read the product contract, not the poster.

## Risks

- Believing “capital guaranteed” language on an investment wrapper.
- Staff incentives to sell higher-fee products without clear risk comparison.
- Rolling maturing deposits automatically into wealth products unless you opt out.

## Literacy angle

Use a simple checklist at the counter:

1. Is this product on my **deposit passbook / deposit certificate**?
2. Who is the **regulator** named in the legal document?
3. Where is the **risk disclosure** signed by me?

If answers are unclear, pause and verify using [NFRA](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/)
consumer materials.

## Official source types

- NFRA and PBOC alerts on financial consumer protection
- Institution-published product factsheets

Links: [PBOC](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/), [NFRA](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/).
