# Postal / bank wealth products vs deposits (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Large banks and postal channels may sell both **deposits** and **wealth/investment** products. Names can sound similar.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households using big-bank or postal counters — literacy is for anyone offered a “higher yield” counter product.

## Liquidity and commitments

Deposits and wealth products have different exit and risk rules.

## Risks

Mistaking an investment product for a guaranteed deposit.

## Literacy angle

Ask: Is principal a deposit? Which regulator? Where is the written risk statement?

## Official source types

Regulator consumer alerts; PBOC; NFRA.

Links: [PBOC](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/), [NFRA](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/).
