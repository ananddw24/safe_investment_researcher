# Government and savings bonds (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Government bonds** and **retail savings-bond style instruments** (when offered
through authorized channels) are ways households may lend to the state or a
designated issuer under published terms. Subscription windows, denominations, and
holding periods are set in official notices — not by social-media “tip groups.”

## Who it is typically designed for

Savers who already maintain accessible emergency cash and who can follow official
subscription calendars and documentation requirements. Bonds are usually a
**longer-horizon literacy topic**, not a substitute for day-to-day liquidity.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Terms vary by issue: fixed maturity, coupon structure, and **secondary-market**
  access (if any) are bond-specific.
- **Early redemption** rules, if offered, are defined in the issue documentation —
  do not assume bank-deposit flexibility.

## Risks

- **Counterfeit or fake “bond collectors”** and unlicensed intermediaries promising
  guaranteed high coupons.
- **Inflation and interest-rate literacy:** nominal coupon vs purchasing power.
- Buying through unofficial apps or messengers without verifying the issuer and
  channel against [MOF](https://www.mof.gov.cn/) or authorized bank notices.

## Literacy angle

Trace every step to an **official announcement** and authorized distributor list.
If someone pressures you to decide today with “exclusive yield,” treat it as a
red flag.

## Official source types

- Ministry of Finance (MOF) bond and fiscal publications
- Authorized commercial bank subscription notices cross-checked with regulator materials

Links: [MOF](https://www.mof.gov.cn/), [PBOC](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/),
[gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/).
