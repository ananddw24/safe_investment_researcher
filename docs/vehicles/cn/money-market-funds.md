# Regulated money market funds (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Money market funds (including products historically associated with Yu’e Bao–style channels) are **regulated fund products**, not bank deposits. Unit values can move.

## Who it is typically designed for

People with emergency cash who understand market/liquidity risk and will verify fund disclosures.

## Liquidity and commitments

Often relatively liquid versus equity funds, but not guaranteed same-day access without rules.

## Risks

Not deposit-insured; yield presentations can mislead; unlicensed copycats.

## Literacy angle

Check that the product is a licensed fund and read the risk disclosure.

## Official source types

CSRC / fund disclosures; PBOC consumer education context.
