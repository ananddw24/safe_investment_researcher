# Regulated money market funds (China)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Money market funds** are **regulated collective investment products**. They
invest in short-term, high-quality instruments under fund rules. Historically,
some households encountered similar liquidity through large payment platforms,
but the literacy point is unchanged: a fund is **not a bank deposit** and unit
values or yields are **not guaranteed** in the same way as deposit principal.

## Who it is typically designed for

People who already hold an emergency buffer in deposits and who will read a
**fund prospectus**, risk disclosure, and redemption rules. Low-income savers
should be especially careful about treating “higher yield” fund marketing as
equivalent to savings accounts.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Redemption is often available on business days, but **not always instant** and
  can be suspended in stress scenarios under fund rules.
- **Large redemptions** may face processing delays depending on the fund and
  channel.
- Platform or bank **wrappers** around a fund can add their own cut-off times.

## Risks

- **No deposit insurance** on fund units — different from
  [bank deposits](bank-deposits.md).
- **Yield presentation** that highlights recent returns without emphasizing
  variability.
- **Unlicensed copycats** mimicking well-known fund or platform names.
- Confusion with informal “wealth management” pools promising fixed high returns —
  see [spotting unregulated schemes](../../guides/spotting-unregulated-schemes.md).

## Literacy angle

Confirm the product is a **licensed public fund** with a disclosed manager and
custodian. Compare liquidity and loss scenarios to a deposit before moving
emergency money.

## Official source types

- CSRC and fund disclosure documents (prospectus, summary)
- PBOC and NFRA consumer education on distinguishing deposits from investments

Links: [PBOC](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/), [NFRA](https://www.nfra.gov.cn/),
[gov.cn](https://www.gov.cn/).
