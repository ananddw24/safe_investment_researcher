# Vehicles

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.

Vehicles are accessible savings and investment *types* for ordinary households,
organized by ISO country code. Pages describe how a type **typically** works —
not which product you should buy.

## How to read these pages

Each vehicle page follows a common outline: what it is, who it is usually for,
liquidity, risks, literacy angles, and where to verify on official sites.

**Depth varies by country:**

| Depth | Countries | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep narrative | India, United States, Kenya | More context on access, fees, and everyday use |
| Short sketch | Nigeria, South Africa, Ghana, Egypt, China, Germany, Japan, United Kingdom, France, Italy, Brazil, Canada | Orientation only — pair with the [resource catalog](../resources/catalog.md) for your country |

If you only read one page in a short country, start with the vehicle that
matches where you already save (often **bank deposits** or **mobile money**).
