# United States — vehicles

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


These pages explain **common regulated** savings and investment *types* for
ordinary U.S. households. They are literacy materials — not product picks.
Start with emergency cash in an insured deposit account before market-linked
products.
