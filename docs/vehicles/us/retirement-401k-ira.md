# 401(k) and IRA concepts

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Employer plans such as **401(k)**-style accounts and individual **IRA** concepts
are long-term retirement savings frameworks under U.S. tax and labor rules.
Contribution limits, withdrawal penalties, and employer matches are rule-based and
change — literacy means learning the vocabulary from IRS and DOL materials, not
picking funds from a tip video.

## Who it is typically designed for

Workers with access to a workplace plan, and people considering an IRA after they
already have emergency savings. Gig and informal workers may lack a workplace plan
and should not confuse social-media “hacks” with official rules.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Liquidity:** low for retirement money — early withdrawals often have taxes and
  penalties as published by the IRS.
- Loans or hardship rules (if any) are plan-specific.

## Risks

- Market-linked investments inside the account can lose value.
- High fees in some options reduce long-term balances.
- Tax mistakes and early withdrawals can be costly.

## Literacy angle

Separate **account type** (401(k)/IRA wrapper) from **investments held inside**.
Read IRS retirement-plan pages before acting on informal advice.

## Official source types

- IRS retirement plans
- U.S. Department of Labor (employee benefits topics)
- CFPB consumer education
