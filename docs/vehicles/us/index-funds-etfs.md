# Low-cost index mutual funds and ETFs (literacy)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Mutual funds and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) pool money into portfolios. **Index**
funds aim to track a market index rather than pick individual stocks. They are
market investments sold through regulated brokers and fund companies — balances can
fall.

## Who it is typically designed for

People with emergency savings who can tolerate price swings and who will verify
fees. Not a first step for money needed within months.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Shares/units can usually be sold on market days, but the price is not guaranteed.
- Brokerage and fund fees reduce net results — learn expense ratios from official
  investor education, not tip channels.

## Risks

- Market loss; past performance does not predict future results.
- Fraudulent “guaranteed return” investment clubs.
- Complex products marketed as “simple indexes.”

## Literacy angle

Use SEC Investor.gov to learn how funds work, how to spot fees, and how to avoid
common scams — not to chase hot tips.

## Official source types

- SEC Investor.gov
- SEC investor education pages
- CFPB (general money tools)
