# Credit union share savings

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Credit unions are member-owned cooperatives. A **share savings** account is a
deposit-like product at a credit union. Share insurance literacy for federal credit
unions is explained by the **NCUA** (distinct from FDIC, with its own published
rules and limits).

## Who it is typically designed for

People who want a cooperative alternative to big banks, including communities that
historically used credit unions for access. Membership eligibility rules apply.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Liquidity:** typically high for basic share savings (institution rules apply).
- Fees and membership requirements vary — read the credit union’s disclosures.

## Risks

- Confusing NCUA and FDIC coverage.
- Fees on small balances.
- Fraud and account takeover (same digital safety habits as banks).

## Literacy angle

Compare insurance agency (NCUA vs FDIC), membership, and fee schedules using
official consumer pages.

## Official source types

- NCUA consumer resources
- CFPB
- FDIC (for contrast with bank insurance)
