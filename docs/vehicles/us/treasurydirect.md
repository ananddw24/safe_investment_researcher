# Treasury securities via TreasuryDirect

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

U.S. Treasury **marketable securities** (such as bills and notes) and certain
savings bonds can be purchased through **TreasuryDirect** and related official
channels. These are government debt instruments with terms published for each
offering — not bank deposits.

## Who it is typically designed for

People who already understand emergency savings and want literacy on holding
government securities for a stated term. Auction and holding mechanics take
learning time.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Bills/notes have stated maturities; selling before maturity (where allowed)
  can mean a price different from purchase.
- Savings bonds have their own holding and redemption rules — read TreasuryDirect.

## Risks

- Interest-rate and inflation can affect real purchasing power and secondary prices.
- Operational mistakes (account access, inheritance, tax reporting) matter.
- Scam sites impersonating TreasuryDirect — use official domains only.

## Literacy angle

Teach auction basics, holding periods, and the difference between Treasury debt
and insured bank deposits.

## Official source types

- TreasuryDirect
- U.S. Treasury / federal consumer education
