# FDIC-insured savings and money market deposit accounts

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

A savings account or money market **deposit** account at an FDIC-insured bank
holds cash you may need soon. Deposit insurance literacy means understanding
coverage limits and what account types are eligible — as published by the FDIC —
not assuming every product labeled “money market” is a bank deposit.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households building an emergency buffer. Low-income savers should watch monthly
fees and minimum-balance rules that can erase small balances.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Liquidity:** typically high for ordinary withdrawals (bank rules and holds apply).
- Debit cards, overdraft, and ATM fees vary — read the bank’s fee schedule.

## Risks

- Uninsured amounts above FDIC limits (ownership categories matter — learn from FDIC materials).
- Fraud, phishing, and account takeover.
- Confusion between deposit accounts and money market **mutual funds** (different risks).

## Literacy angle

Teach insurance coverage, fee reading, and “emergency cash before investing.”
