# SEC-registered money market and mutual funds (Nigeria)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Money market and mutual funds sold to the public should be registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) Nigeria. Unit values can move — these are not bank deposits.

## Who it is typically designed for

People with emergency cash who will verify SEC registration before contributing.

## Liquidity and commitments

Fund-specific redemption rules; NAV can fall.

## Risks

Unlicensed copycats; “guaranteed fund returns”; fee drag.

## Literacy angle

Make licensing checks a habit; use SEC Nigeria materials.

## Official source types

SEC Nigeria; CBN consumer education context.

Links: [SEC Nigeria](https://sec.gov.ng/), [CBN](https://www.cbn.gov.ng/).
