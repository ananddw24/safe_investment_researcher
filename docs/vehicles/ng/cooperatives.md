# Cooperatives and thrift societies (Nigeria)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Cooperatives and thrift societies are common group savings/credit structures. Governance quality varies widely.

## Who it is typically designed for

Workers and community groups, including women-led thrifts — literacy focuses on rules and oversight.

## Liquidity and commitments

Notice periods and loan obligations are group-specific.

## Risks

Poor governance, fraud, and coercive lending; confusing thrifts with licensed banks.

## Literacy angle

Ask for written bylaws, leadership accountability, and whether any CBN-licensed entity is involved.

## Official source types

CBN guidance context.
