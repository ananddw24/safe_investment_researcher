# Treasury bills and FGN bonds (Nigeria)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Treasury bills and Federal Government of Nigeria (FGN) bonds are government debt. Retail access channels change — literacy covers inflation and FX context for capital preservation, not trade ideas.

## Who it is typically designed for

Savers who already have emergency cash and can follow official Debt Management Office (DMO) / CBN materials.

## Liquidity and commitments

Holding periods and secondary-market prices depend on the instrument.

## Risks

Inflation and FX stress can affect real outcomes; fake “government bond” collectors.

## Literacy angle

Use DMO and CBN publications; ignore WhatsApp “sure FGN” tips.

## Official source types

DMO; CBN.
