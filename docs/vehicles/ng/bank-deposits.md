# Bank savings and fixed deposits (Nigeria)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Bank savings and fixed deposits are core regulated household channels under Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) consumer-protection framing.

## Who it is typically designed for

Households needing a formal cash buffer; low-income savers should watch fees that erode small balances.

## Liquidity and commitments

Savings: typically higher access. Fixed deposits: term commitments and break rules may apply.

## Risks

Fees, fraud, and confusing deposits with unregulated “investment clubs.” Inflation can reduce purchasing power even when nominal rates look positive.

## Literacy angle

Build emergency cash in a licensed bank before long lock-ins; read fee schedules.

## Official source types

CBN.
