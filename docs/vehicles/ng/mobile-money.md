# Mobile money and fintech wallet savings features (Nigeria)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Mobile money and fintech wallets may offer savings-like features. Payment float is not the same as an insured bank deposit — CBN payment-system rules are the literacy anchor.

## Who it is typically designed for

People needing everyday payments and small balances, including first-time formal users.

## Liquidity and commitments

Typically high for wallet balances; lock features reduce access.

## Risks

Fraud, SIM-swap, unregulated “investment” apps riding on wallet brands.

## Literacy angle

Check licensing and fee tables; separate payment float from emergency deposits.

## Official source types

CBN.
