# Contributory Pension System concepts (Nigeria)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Nigeria’s Contributory Pension System concepts cover formal-worker retirement savings under PenCom oversight. Informal workers often have coverage gaps.

## Who it is typically designed for

Formal employees; literacy includes knowing who is *not* automatically covered.

## Liquidity and commitments

Low — long horizon with restricted access under scheme rules.

## Risks

Incomplete contributions; informal “pension clubs”; market-linked components where applicable.

## Literacy angle

Verify on PenCom official materials before tip videos.

## Official source types

PenCom.
