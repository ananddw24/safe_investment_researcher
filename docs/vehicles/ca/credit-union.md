# Credit union deposits (Canada)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

Credit union deposits are cooperative alternatives; provincial deposit insurance regimes differ from CDIC.

## Who it is typically designed for

Members seeking cooperative banking access.

## Liquidity and commitments

Typically high for basic savings; membership rules apply.

## Risks

Confusing provincial insurance with CDIC; fees.

## Literacy angle

Ask which insurer covers the credit union; use FCAC materials.

## Official source types

FCAC; CDIC (for contrast).

Links: [FCAC](https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency.html), [CDIC](https://www.cdic.ca/).
