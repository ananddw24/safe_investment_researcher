# Credit union deposits (Canada)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

**Credit unions** (caisses populaires in Quebec) are member-owned cooperatives offering
chequing, savings, loans, and sometimes wealth services. Deposits are **not CDIC-insured**
in the federal sense; most provinces use **provincial deposit insurers** (for example
FSRA deposit insurance in Ontario, CDIC does not apply — verify your province).

## Who it is typically designed for

Members meeting **common bond** rules (region, employer, association). Often competitive
HISA rates and community branches.

## Liquidity and commitments

- Everyday savings: typically flexible; read **share vs savings** account types.
- **Membership shares** may have different withdrawal rules than deposit accounts.

## Risks

- Assuming **CDIC** covers a credit union because the logo looks “bank-like.”
- **Uninsured** amounts above provincial limits.
- Fraud via Interac like any payment institution.

## Literacy angle

Ask the credit union for the **deposit insurer name and limit** in writing. FCAC explains
differences between banks and credit unions at a high level.

## Official source types

- FCAC banking guides
- Provincial regulator and deposit insurer pages (varies by province)

Links: [FCAC](https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency.html),
