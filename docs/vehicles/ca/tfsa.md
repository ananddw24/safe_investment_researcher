## What it is

A Tax-Free Savings Account is a tax wrapper with contribution room rules — literacy not product pick.

## Who it is typically designed for

Residents learning contribution room after emergency cash exists.

## Liquidity and commitments

Depends on investments held inside; cash TFSAs differ from invested TFSAs.

## Risks

Over-contribution penalties; market loss if invested.
