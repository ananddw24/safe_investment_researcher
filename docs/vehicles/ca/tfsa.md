# TFSA concepts (Canada)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

A **Tax-Free Savings Account (TFSA)** is a **registered tax wrapper**. Growth and
qualified withdrawals are tax-free under rules set by CRA and explained for consumers
on Canada.ca/FCAC. You can hold **cash**, **GICs**, **mutual funds**, **ETFs**, or
other eligible assets inside — the wrapper does not remove **investment risk**.

## Who it is typically designed for

Canadian residents age 18+ with **contribution room** accumulated since 2009 or year
of eligibility. Low-income savers should learn how TFSA withdrawals interact with
**income-tested benefits** using official benefit pages, not Reddit threads.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Cash TFSA:** generally withdraw anytime; re-contribute room returns **next calendar year**
  unless you wait — over-contributing triggers penalties.
- **Invested TFSA:** selling at a loss is possible; settlement delays apply.

## Risks

- **Over-contribution** penalty tax from misunderstanding room after withdrawals.
- Treating TFSA as day-trading account without market loss literacy.
- Scams promising “CRA TFSA unlock” via gift cards.

## Literacy angle

Check **My Account** CRA room before large deposits. FCAC explains TFSA vs RRSP roles
at a high level — this site does not tax-plan for individuals.

## Official source types

- FCAC TFSA guides
- Canada.ca CRA TFSA room pages (via official search)
