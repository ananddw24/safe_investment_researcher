# RRSP concepts (Canada)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

A **Registered Retirement Savings Plan (RRSP)** is a **tax-deferred** retirement
account: contributions may reduce taxable income within limits; withdrawals are generally
taxable. You can hold cash, GICs, funds, or other qualified investments inside.
**Home Buyers’ Plan** and **Lifelong Learning Plan** are special borrow-your-own-RRSP
programs with repayment rules — verify on CRA pages.

## Who it is typically designed for

Earners with room who are saving for **retirement** or approved HBP/LLP uses. Poor
vehicle for emergency cash because withdrawal triggers withholding and tax reporting.

## Liquidity and commitments

- **Low** for emergencies; **HBP/LLP** reintroduce structured repayment obligations.
- **RRSP maturity** converts to RRIF or annuity later — long arc literacy.

## Risks

- Raiding RRSP for consumption and losing compounding plus paying tax.
- Holding high-fee mutual funds because of bank default menus.
- Confusing **DPSP** employer plans with personal RRSP room.
