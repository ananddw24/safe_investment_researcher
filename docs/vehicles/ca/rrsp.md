# RRSP concepts (Canada)

!!! warning "Educational only"
    This page is for financial literacy. It is **not** personalized advice,
    an offer to sell, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any product.
    Rules differ by country; verify with official sources in your jurisdiction.


## What it is

RRSPs are retirement savings concepts with tax-deferral features and contribution limits under Canadian rules.

## Who it is typically designed for

People planning long-horizon retirement savings.

## Liquidity and commitments

Withdrawals can have tax consequences; literacy first.

## Risks

Early withdrawals; market loss; contribution errors.

## Literacy angle

Use FCAC / Canada.ca guidance; confirm CRA details on official pages when available.
