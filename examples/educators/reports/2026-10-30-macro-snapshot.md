Readings from the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (FRED). Only observations on or
before 2026-10-30 are used. The FOMC sets the target range (`DFEDTARL`–`DFEDTARU`).
`FEDFUNDS` is the monthly average effective rate in the market, not the target. A
12-month CPI percent change here is computed from the index levels; it is not a separate
published percent series. Pair the page with the library records `us-federal-reserve`,
`us-fdic-deposit-insurance`, and `us-cfpb`.
